Speed is useful, but not every part of engineering deserves the same speed.

## Hidden decisions

Many tasks look mechanical until they touch a boundary: data ownership, user trust, deployment risk, or an API that other people will build against.

## Better pace

Move quickly through reversible work. Slow down when the cost of being wrong compounds.

## Practice

Before changing a system, ask what future reader will assume from the shape you leave behind.
