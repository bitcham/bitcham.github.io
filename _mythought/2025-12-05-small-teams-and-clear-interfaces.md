Small teams do not get speed from everyone knowing everything. They get speed from reducing the number of surprises.

## Human interfaces

A good interface between people says what can be changed freely, what needs review, and what must never be hidden.

## The software mirror

This is close to backend design. A module with clear inputs, outputs, and failure modes lets another person build nearby without holding the entire system in their head.

## Working habit

Make ownership visible. Then make handoffs smaller than they feel.
