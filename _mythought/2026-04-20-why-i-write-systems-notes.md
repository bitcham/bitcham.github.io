I write systems notes because memory is too generous. It smooths over the awkward parts of a decision and keeps only the clean story.

## A note is a trace

Good notes preserve the shape of uncertainty. They show what was known, what was guessed, and what would change the decision later.

## Engineering benefit

The best part is not documentation. The best part is that writing makes fuzzy thinking easier to catch while it is still cheap to change.

## Personal rule

If a decision might matter twice, it deserves a note.
