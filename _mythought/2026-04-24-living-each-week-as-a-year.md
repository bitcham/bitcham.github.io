These days, I’m living each week as if it were a year.

From the moment I open my eyes in the morning until I close them at night, I’m constantly learning, thinking, trying to keep up with new technologies, while also building the fundamentals so I can understand things at a deeper level.

But the world is changing so fast that even this feels insufficient.

At the same time, I sometimes wonder whether I’m only seeing a narrow slice of the world.

AI is turning the world upside down.

What if, with my limited perspective, I draw the life I thought I wanted, only to find that when everyone else leaves the land and moves out to sea, I’m left standing there alone?
